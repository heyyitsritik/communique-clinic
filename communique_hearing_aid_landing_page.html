Frequently asked questions

Do I need a hearing test before buying hearing aids?

Yes. A hearing aid should be selected after a professional hearing assessment so that amplification matches your hearing loss and listening needs.

Can I try hearing aids before purchase?

Yes. Suitable hearing aids can be demonstrated so you can understand comfort, clarity and handling before making a decision.

Which type of hearing aid is best?

The best type depends on your hearing loss, ear canal size, lifestyle, cosmetic preference, dexterity and budget. RIC, BTE, CIC and IIC styles all have different advantages.

Do you provide after-fitting support?

Yes. Follow-up fine-tuning, counselling, cleaning guidance and troubleshooting support are important parts of successful hearing aid use.

Do you also manage tinnitus and vertigo?

Yes. The clinic provides tinnitus counselling and advanced vestibular assessment including VNG, vHIT, VEMP, SVV and vestibular rehabilitation guidance.
